Methodology and limitations

  • Paired decision-only ablation on fixed synthetic traces generated by the full harness. No alternative agent behavior is simulated.
  • S0 is an acceptance-only proxy, not a measurement of GitHub CI or any commercial product. No LLM reviewer is benchmarked.
  • ALLOW means hypothetical eligibility, never permission to merge. S4 reproduces the independently replayed full decision.
  • Missing evidence fails closed. Pre-execution proposal rejections do not demonstrate that baseline CI detects scope violations.
  • Each hand-designed scenario has equal weight. Rates are not estimates of production incident probabilities.
  • Fixture signing keys, reviewers and observations are synthetic. No inference, token cost, human time or throughput was measured.
  • Observed work is shared across strategies. No counterfactual execution cost or loop savings are estimated.
